System Architecture

The Decoupled Three-Tier Architecture

Separating physical camera streams from computer vision inference and business logic enables infinite scalability and true vendor independence.

LAYER 01
Hardware Ingestion
Any RTSP / ONVIF IP camera stream. Zero vendor lock-in. Health telemetry & offline heartbeat monitoring.
➔
LAYER 02
AI Computer Vision
YOLOv8/v10 spatial tracking + posture estimation. Probabilistic confidence scoring. Polygon zone mapping.
➔
LAYER 03
Control Center UI
Policy configuration, real-time occupancy curves, human-in-the-loop triage, and immutable audit logs.
Why Decoupling Matters
Traditional surveillance ties the software to the camera brand (Axis, Hikvision, Dahua). SmartCCTV treats any IP camera as a generic sensor stream, executing uniform AI models on the cloud or edge gateway.
Edge-to-Cloud Hybrid Model
Bandwidth-friendly: cameras only transmit metadata and lightweight event clips when movement or threshold breaches occur, slashing cloud bandwidth bills by 85%.

Ethics, Privacy & Verification

Responsible AI: Human-in-the-Loop Triage

Computer vision models are probabilistic. SmartCCTV guarantees operational integrity by pairing automated detections with human operator review.

0%
Biometric Identification
Strictly privacy-preserving: tracking bounding volumes and anonymous posture coordinates, never human faces.
100%
Raw Output Integrity
Human review never overwrites raw model outputs or confidence scores—preserving true forensic auditability.
3-State
Triage Verification
Confirmed · False Positive · Needs Further Review, accompanied by timestamped operator notes.
The Ethical Engineering Philosophy
AI should surface structured signals to empower human managers, not execute autonomous punishments. If an employee is holding an inhaler or calculator, our 3-state review allows an operator to label it a False Positive in seconds, simultaneously generating training data to fine-tune future model accuracy.
